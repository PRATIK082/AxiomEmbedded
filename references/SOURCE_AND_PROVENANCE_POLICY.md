# Source, provenance, and clean-room policy

## Purpose

This repository accepts only material that contributors have the right to submit.

## Allowed inputs

1. Original work created for this repository.
2. Material under an identified compatible open-source, open-data, open-hardware, or open-documentation license.
3. Public-domain material, only when the public-domain status is documented.
4. Facts, ideas, processes, and interoperability observations restated independently without copying protected expression.
5. Short quotations when necessary, attributed, license-compatible, and reviewed.
6. Links and bibliographic references to external standards or documentation without copying restricted content.

## Prohibited inputs

- Employer, customer, supplier, partner, or previous-project source code, requirements, architecture, tests, reports, configurations, screenshots, datasets, models, credentials, or internal knowledge that is confidential or proprietary.
- Text, figures, tables, checklists, rule wording, examples, or mappings copied from paid or restricted standards.
- Content from publicly viewable websites when no reuse license permits incorporation.
- Decompiled, leaked, NDA-controlled, export-controlled, classified, personal, or security-sensitive material.
- AI-generated content based on proprietary prompts, files, repositories, or undisclosed restricted sources.
- Copied trademarked logos or branding unless separately authorized.

## Clean-room rule development

1. Record only the standard name, edition, official URL, and clause identifier where permitted.
