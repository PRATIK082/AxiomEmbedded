# Axiom Safety I/O Matrix Review

## Purpose
Independently reviews the safety I/O matrix for safety i/o matrix work. Maps safety inputs and outputs to controllers: test pulses, discrepancy monitoring, and proof-test intervals.
This is the checklist reviewer partner of the builder skill; it never modifies the artifact, it records a verdict.

## When to Use
- The project needs the safety i/o matrix review workflow.
- Inputs listed below are available.

## When Not to Use
- As a substitute for qualified safety/security certification review.
- When the required inputs are absent (record the gap instead of inventing data).

## Required Inputs
- safety-function list.
- controller spec.
- wiring design.


## Review Workflow
1. Confirm the builder artifact exists and matches its template.
2. Apply each checklist gate; sample evidence, do not trust assertions.
3. Audit traceability links end to end.
4. Record verdict: pass, pass-with-findings, or fail, with severity-ranked findings.
5. Record reviewer independence and hand back open questions.

## Builder + reviewer pairing

This skill is the reviewer partner of `axiom-safety-io-matrix`. It must not modify the builder artifact; it samples evidence, records findings with severity, and emits a verdict. Independence of the reviewer is a release gate.

## Three-zone autonomy

Autonomy is zoned by risk; the V&V boundary is the automation boundary.

- Core / evidence zone (AI no access, human accountable): certified or
  qualified artifacts, evidence records, safety/security-critical code, and
  release baselines. AI must not read or write here without an explicit
  human-approved change request.
- Gray zone (AI assistant + HITL): AI plans and drafts; a human decides and
  approves each step. Architecture, requirements, tests with safety impact, and
  verification-affecting configuration live here.
- Non-critical zone (AI unrestricted): documentation, examples, scaffolding,
  low-risk refactors, and tooling glue.

This skill's outputs are treated as gray-zone by default; AI may draft but a
human approves. AI must never act autonomously in the core/evidence zone.
