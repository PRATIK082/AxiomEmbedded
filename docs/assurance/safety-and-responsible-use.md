# Safety and responsible-use policy

1. Assurance output is draft engineering evidence for qualified human review.
   It is never a certification, conformity approval, safety case verdict, or
   security certificate.
2. Formal conformity depends on the applicable regulatory scheme and
   authorized assessment; the adopting project owns editions, objectives,
   independence, and evidence qualification.
3. Never fabricate measurements, test results, failure rates, supplier data,
   or approvals. Mark gaps UNKNOWN or OPEN with an owner.
4. Reviewers never silently alter builder output; reports are separate and
   append-only.
5. Banned phrasing: "certified", "proves compliance", "replaces a notified
   body/assessor". Use: "supports engineering assessment", "draft evidence
   requiring qualified human review".
